Brushstrokes of Nature

A collection of my paintings and the stories they tell

Welcome to my artistic journey👋

By day, I'm an engineer solving complex problems with logic and precision. But when the sun sets, I trade my keyboard for a paintbrush and let nature's endless symphony guide my hand. Every hiking trail whispers a story, every mountain peak holds a secret, and every forest clearing becomes my muse. The great outdoors isn't just my escape—it's my greatest teacher, showing me colors I never knew existed and moments of pure magic that I'm compelled to capture on canvas.

~ Sanjana, Engineer & Nature's Student
